PROJECT OVERVIEW

Powering a Corporate Giant

Aether Clean Energy sought to completely overhaul their corporate headquarters' energy consumption model. As a growing manufacturing and tech hub, their grid dependency resulted in excessive operational costs and a carbon footprint that misaligned with their corporate sustainability goals.

The Challenge

The primary challenge was installing a high-capacity system on a multi-level, complex roof structure without disrupting the 24/7 operations of the facility. Additionally, the local grid suffered from frequent voltage fluctuations, requiring a robust battery backup and intelligent inverter system to ensure smooth transition during outages.

The Bright Power Solution

Our engineering team designed a custom 500 kWp solar array utilizing high-efficiency monocrystalline panels. To address the grid instability, we integrated a state-of-the-art 200 kWh battery storage system alongside smart microinverters. The installation was phased over three weekends to ensure zero downtime for the client.

Environmental Impact

The system currently offsets 85% of the building's daytime energy usage. Annually, this translates to a reduction of over 400 metric tons of CO2 emissions—the equivalent of planting 6,500 trees or taking 85 cars off the road every single year.
