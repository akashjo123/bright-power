Elamakkara Solar Installation

A high-efficiency grid-tied solar system powering a residential property in Kochi.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Reliable Grid-Tied Power

This residential project in Elamakkara, Kochi was designed to maximize energy savings by utilizing an advanced grid-tied architecture. The homeowner wanted a clean, low-maintenance setup that seamlessly integrates with the Kerala State Electricity Board (KSEB) grid to offset monthly energy bills.

Eastman Grid Tie Inverter Setup
Lat 10.022129° Long 76.29207°  |  Monday, 20/07/2026 05:19 PM GMT +05:30

Hardware & Installation

The roof structure required a customized mounting approach. We deployed high-efficiency solar modules directly onto the angled red metal roof, ensuring optimal tilt for maximum solar yield. The energy collected is fed directly into a state-of-the-art Eastman Grid Tie Inverter, which safely synchronizes the solar power with the home's electrical panel and the utility grid.
