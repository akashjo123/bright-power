PROJECT OVERVIEW

Ayroor, Pathanamthitta

A reliable residential power solution powered by a MICROTEK inverter system. This installation guarantees stable performance and excellent energy savings in a verdant, semi-urban environment in Ayroor, Kerala.

Hardware & Installation

For the Ayroor site, we utilized an advanced MICROTEK Grid Tie Inverter to manage the load effectively. The solar panels are securely mounted on the sloped roof, capturing optimal sunlight to offset the property's grid reliance.
